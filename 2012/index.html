2012

2012 is a six minute animated short directed by Ingo Raschka, made using hand painted acetate cels photographed over colored pencil backgrounds. Cel animation was once the most commonly used animation process worldwide, and was succeeded by digital paint techniques around the turn of the century.

2012 is an uncomfortable story about failing to find love in the face of death.

This film was made possible by the tireless help of my crew: Sam Maurer, Noah Malone, Alvin Fai, Mabel Ye, Zach Marcus, Chris and Lydie Raschka, Hedda Durnbaugh, Yonatan Tal, and Josh Swallow.

2012 premiered at Ottawa International Film Festival in 2017 and has since screened at Cactus Club, VIDEODOME, and the Anthology Film Archives. 2012 won the Toronto Audience Award at the 2023 Transfiguration International Film Festival.
