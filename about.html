inga waste (aka ingo raschka) is a new york native. born and raised in uptown Manhattan, inga has been making animation from a young age. she grew up making claymation inspired by rex the runt, bill plympton, dexter's lab, magic school bus, powerpuff girls, early CGI experiments, in college, she transitioned to making hand-drawn mixed media animation. her college thesis was 2012, an apocalypse anxiety love story made with hand-painted cels. she utilized her college print lab to make hand-painted cel animation. since then, she has worked both in-house and freelance animation jobs.
