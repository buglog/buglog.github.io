Human Animal Alphabet featured works by myself, Pamela Anderson, Marlene Frontera, and Krystine Kryttre. The show was up between September 17 and October 20th at the Frog Skateboard Shop in Frogtown, Los Angeles. The sculptures I showed were concieved as physical memories. I call them "soft collage" or "touch collage." Each time the works are installed, the pieces inside shift around to form a new image. Some of the collages are shaped like dollhouses.
