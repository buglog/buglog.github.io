Human Animal Alphabet featured works by myself, Pamela Anderson, Marlene Frontera, and Krystine Kryttre. The show was up between September 17 and October 20th at the Frog Skateboard Shop in Frogtown, Los Angeles.

The sculptures I showed were concieved as physical memories. I call them "soft collage" or "touch collage." The pieces consist of plastic envelopes, some abstract and some shaped like houses. Each envelope is filled with detritus, mostly collected from the ground and the refuse in my house.

Each time the works are installed, the pieces inside shift around to form a new image. It forms an analogy with memory in the sense that each time a memory is recalled, it is different. Some say that this is exemplary of the fallibility of human memory. In my case, i believe that a memory can remain accurate each time it is recalled, even though it may change radically from it's initial form. This is because the nature of experience is so complex that it may take a thousand different memories of the same instance to truly comprehend what really happened. Our experiences are formed of a fusion of fact and fiction. Memories are neither.
