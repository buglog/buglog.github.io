Golden Bullet of L.O.V.E. was made in 72 hours as a mixed-media collaboration between myself and animator Luca Depardon. We collaborated long-distance. I was in California and he in Rhode Island. We came up with the concept together, I made the storyboards and then we made our shots separately. We both used hand-painted cels in our shots. For me, the practice of using cels was a precursor to the film i would produce the next year, 2012. You can watch the film here.
