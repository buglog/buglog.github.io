Brian's Pans

July 4, 2019

When researching animated pans I asked my friend Brian Kim for some references. He sent me this nice and informative email, which I'm publishing here in full with his permission. Brian is an amazing animator with an encyclopedic knowledge of animation and anime, making him a great resource for anyone interested in learning more about specific types of shots or techniques in animation. You can look at Brian's work Here.
