XSHEETS! ①

June 4, 2020

This is the first of a series of posts about xsheets, or exposure sheets, and the role they play in animation.

In the series, i will tell you what xsheets are, what they're used for, what parts of the process they affect, and how to make your own. (spoiler alert– xsheets are just timelines). Later i will discuss how xsheets relate to the rest of the process, and how xsheets can be used creatively as an interface to compose animation like a musical score. Part 2 will feature a discussion with Castlevania animation director Spencer Wan.
